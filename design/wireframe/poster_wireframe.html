Design notes

Mid-fidelity wireframe · A2 portrait (420 × 594 mm) at 2.4 px/mm · real data, final colours, draft copy · images are placeholders.

  1. A · Header. Title, one-line subtitle, and a lede: the whole story in one sentence for someone who reads nothing else. The 46 logo and the helmet sit top-right as images.
  2. B · Map (the hero). Mugello turned 90° like TV graphics, so the main straight runs across. Only the corners where something happened get a numbered badge. Yellow badge = a Rossi moment, outlined badge = context (e.g. Stoner's crash). The green bar marks the finish line. All 15 corner names are kept small because the track itself is a protagonist.
  3. C · Story cards. One card per key moment, in lap order: lap, headline, two or three plain sentences (≤ 200 characters, enforced by a test). A thin curve leads from each card to its lap on the axis.
  4. D · Laps + positions. One shared lap axis (grid → lap 23). The badges sit on the axis and faint guides drop into the position chart below. Position chart: 1st at the top, only the leading 6 riders, Rossi thick yellow, Capirossi coral, everyone else thin grey and unlabelled. Labels at the line ends name both riders, so colour is never the only cue.
  5. E · Key numbers. Three big figures with a plain label each. Same font as everything else, proportional digits. Credits line (required by the track licence).

Reading path

Title → lede → map badges → cards ① to ⑦ → the yellow line falling to 5th and climbing back → the three numbers.
